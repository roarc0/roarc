Privacy Policy

App: Whitelies
Package: com.roarc.whitelies
Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Overview

Whitelies is a local multiplayer party game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, usage analytics, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to discover and connect to nearby devices for local multiplayer gameplay. Bluetooth is used solely for device-to-device communication within the same game session. No data is transmitted to any server or third party.

4. Advertising

The app may display ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You can review Google's data practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Local Storage

Game settings and word lists are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
